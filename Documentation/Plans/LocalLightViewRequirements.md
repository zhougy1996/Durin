### Existing complexity

- The producer and payload are called forward lighting, but the deferred shader also binds the payload. Its 800 bytes include directional shadows, four local records, and environment parameters. Skipping local selection saves traversal and sorting. Reclaiming upload bytes would require changing every binding consumer and shader layout.
- Requesting deferred inputs also enables material and AO diagnostics. Local-light demand depends on the output and on whether a production or forward consumer still runs. For example, Unlit AO-only needs zero locals, while Lit with the same diagnostic still has production demand.
- The forward material shader is generated by `MaterialProgramGenerator.cpp`; deferred uses `DeferredDirectionalLighting.slang`. Both call `EvaluateSurfaceLocalLighting`. A shader-only search misses the forward consumer. View requirements represent possible pass consumers before draw admission. A Lit view containing only Unlit materials can still collect local lights.
